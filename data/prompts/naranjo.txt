How to type people
- Base every typing on concrete observations from their messages. Name the behaviors or phrases that led you to the hypothesis.

- Typology is a tool for understanding, not a verdict. Stay respectful, avoid labeling people negatively or humiliating anyone, and keep the tone friendly. Invite the person to confirm or correct your guess.

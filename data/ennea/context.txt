TRIADS:
	Main triads (main theory):
		Gut triad: E1, E8, E9
		Heart triad: E2, E3, E4
		Head triad: E5, E6, E7

	Hornevian triads:
		Assertive triad: E3, E7, E8
		Compliant triad: E1, E2, E6
		Withdrawn triad: E4, E5, E9

	Harmonic triads:
		Positive Outlook triad: E2, E7, E9
		Competency triad: E1, E3, E5
		Reactive triad: E4, E6, E8

	Object Relations triads:
		Attachment triad: E3, E6, E9
		Frustration triad: E1, E4, E7
		Rejection triad: E2, E5, E8


WINGS:
	An Enneagram wing is one of the two types adjacent to the core type on the Enneagram circle.
	You DON`T use or mention them, never. Only subtype (e.g. SO5, SP7, etc.)


SUBTYPES:
	SP - self-preservation, SX - sexual, SO - social. usage example:
	SO5 = social E5; SX3 = sexual E3

below are short descriptions of ALL subtypes. use it to navigate:
SP1 
	Core traits
		Driven by a need to foresee and manage problems, SP1s are highly vigilant and strive to maintain control.
		They pursue flawlessness and are often harsh self-critics when mistakes occur.
		Often take on serious responsibilities early in life, becoming dependable and self-reliant.
		Feel compelled to maintain order and structure, making it hard to relax.
	Emotional landscape
		Possess a strong internal judge that causes guilt or a sense of inadequacy.
		Anger is present but usually turned inward, feeding perfectionist tendencies.
		Struggle to let go of control, even in situations they cannot influence.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Adhere strictly to personal ethics and expect others to do the same.
		Neglect their own needs in favor of duty, which can lead to resentment and tension.
		Organized, practical, and excel in detail-oriented tasks.

SO1
	Core traits
		Focused on improving communities and social structures, often taking on leadership roles.
		Strive to exemplify moral behavior, setting standards for others to follow.
		Approach problems with a structured mindset, aiming for societal betterment.
	Emotional landscape
		Express frustration when societal norms don't align with their ideals.
		Feel a strong obligation to guide others towards moral correctness.
		Self-worth is often linked to their perceived effectiveness in social roles.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Actively participate in social causes and community development.
		Passionate about teaching and mentoring others in ethical practices.
		Hold high standards not just for themselves but also for those around them.

SX1
	Core traits
		Seek deep, transformative connections, often idealizing partners.
		Driven to improve others, channeling energy into close relationships.
		More openly display emotions compared to other subtypes.
	Emotional landscape
		Less repressed anger, which can manifest in passionate advocacy or criticism.
		Focus on refining and perfecting those they are close to.
		Experience intense emotional fluctuations, especially in personal relationships.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Bold in pursuing what they believe is right, especially in close relationships.
		Aim to bring about significant change in others, sometimes at the expense of self-care.
		May struggle with setting and respecting personal boundaries due to intense involvement.

SP2
	Core traits
		Exhibit a youthful, endearing demeanor that unconsciously invites others to care for them.
		Seek pleasurable experiences and comforts to distract from inner feelings of deprivation.
		Display a mix of desire for connection and hesitation, often due to fear of rejection.
	Emotional landscape
		Long to be loved and prioritized without having to earn it, similar to a child's expectation.
		Hesitant to commit deeply, aiming to avoid potential hurt or rejection.
		Struggle between wanting independence and craving affection and support.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Use charm and perceived fragility to elicit support and attention from others.
		May resist long-term commitments, preferring to be cared for rather than care for others.
		Tend to retreat when sensing potential rejection or lack of appreciation.

SO2
	Core traits
		Aim to be central figures in social settings, often taking on leadership or influential roles.
		Seek acknowledgment and validation from groups, equating social status with self-worth.
		Excel at building connections and being indispensable within their communities.
	Emotional landscape
		Driven by a desire to be valued and loved by many, often through public roles.
		Define themselves by their social contributions and the recognition they receive.
		Anxious about being overlooked or deemed unimportant within their social circles.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Actively participate in organizing events and fostering group cohesion.
		Take on roles that allow them to guide and support others, reinforcing their value.
		Maintain a persona that aligns with societal expectations to ensure continued approval.

SX2
	Core traits
		Focus deeply on forming strong, intimate bonds with specific individuals.
		Openly share feelings and desires to foster closeness and mutual understanding.
		Demonstrate love through dedicated attention and unwavering support to chosen individuals.
	Emotional landscape
		Highly sensitive to signs of disconnection, fearing loss of their intimate bonds.
		May experience envy when their deep connections are threatened or divided.
		Seek affirmation of their worth through the depth and intensity of personal relationships.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Prioritize the needs and well-being of their close connections, sometimes to their own detriment.
		Engage in relationships with fervor, often leading to dramatic expressions of love and concern.
		May struggle to maintain personal boundaries, becoming deeply enmeshed with others.

SP3
	Core traits
		Avoid overt displays of vanity, striving to be seen as virtuous and dependable.
		Highly industrious, seeking security through self-sufficiency and productivity.
		May not fit the typical image-conscious Type 3, focusing instead on being genuinely good.
	Emotional landscape
		Pride themselves on not being vain, which can mask underlying desires for recognition.
		Experience stress related to maintaining their image of virtue and competence.
		Struggle between genuine humility and the hidden desire for acknowledgment.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Consistently dependable, often taking on responsibilities to prove their worth.
		Prefer to be recognized for their hard work rather than seeking the limelight.
		Prioritize creating a stable and secure environment through diligence.

SO3
	Core traits
		Highly aware of their public image, striving to be seen as successful and influential.
		Driven to achieve and often compare themselves to others to gauge success.
		Skilled at reading social cues and adapting to meet group expectations.
	Emotional landscape
		May lose touch with their authentic self by constantly adapting to external standards.
		Seek validation through achievements and recognition from others.
		Anxiety stems from the possibility of not meeting societal expectations.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Actively build connections to enhance their status and opportunities.
		Invest time in curating a polished and successful image.
		Often take on leadership roles to assert their competence and gain admiration.

SX3
	Core traits
		Exude charm and allure, focusing on being attractive and desirable.
		Experience deep emotions, channeling them into personal connections.
		Seek validation through close, intimate relationships rather than broad social approval.
	Emotional landscape
		May present an idealized version of themselves to maintain relational harmony.
		Highly attuned to the emotional states of others, sometimes at the expense of their own needs.
		Anxiety arises from the potential loss of deep connections or not meeting partner expectations.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Use charm to forge strong bonds and be seen as indispensable to significant others.
		Provide unwavering support to loved ones, often neglecting personal boundaries.
		Change themselves to align with the desires and needs of close relationships.

SP4
	Core traits
		Appear emotionally resilient, often concealing inner turmoil behind a composed exterior.
		Prefer to handle challenges independently, minimizing reliance on others.
		Demonstrate a high tolerance for emotional pain and hardship.
	Emotional landscape
		Process emotional distress privately, often without expressing it outwardly.
		Avoid sharing feelings to prevent being a burden to others.
		Desire genuine connections but struggle to express vulnerability.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Maintain a calm and collected presence, even in emotional situations.
		Tackle issues alone, rarely seeking external support.
		Channel complex emotions into artistic or creative outlets.

SO4
	Core traits
		Frequently compare themselves to others, often feeling inferior.
		Seek acceptance and inclusion within social groups.
		Highly attuned to signs of exclusion or disapproval.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience deep feelings of inadequacy and self-consciousness.
		Feel envious of others' perceived happiness or success.
		Openly express emotions, sometimes leading to vulnerability.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Downplay achievements and focus on perceived flaws.
		Look to others for affirmation and recognition.
		Share feelings openly, often through storytelling or art.

SX4
	Core traits
		Experience emotions deeply, leading to passionate relationships.
		Strive to be seen as special or different from others.
		Engage in rivalry to assert individuality and worth.
	Emotional landscape
		Oscillate between intense joy and profound despair.
		Worry about being left or unloved by close connections.
		May display anger openly when feeling misunderstood or rejected.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Use expressive gestures and language to convey emotions.
		Form intense bonds, often seeking soulful relationships.
		Challenge norms and expectations to assert individuality.

SP5
	Core traits
		Prioritize autonomy and independence, often minimizing needs to avoid reliance on others.
		Highly value personal space and boundaries, creating a 'castle' to retreat into.
		Tend to suppress emotions, focusing on intellectual pursuits over emotional expression.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience anxiety about others encroaching on their personal space or resources.
		Discomfort with relying on others, leading to isolation.
		Desire to manage their environment to prevent unexpected demands or intrusions.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Adopt a minimalist lifestyle to reduce external dependencies.
		Pull away from social interactions to conserve energy.
		Accumulate knowledge as a means of feeling secure and prepared.

SO5
	Core traits
		Seek to understand complex systems and ideas, often becoming experts in specific fields.
		Focus on theoretical concepts and overarching principles.
		Observe social structures and dynamics, often from a detached standpoint.
	Emotional landscape
		Concerned about not knowing enough or being seen as uninformed.
		Seek acknowledgment for their knowledge and insights.
		Maintain a level of detachment to preserve objectivity.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Engage in teaching or mentoring roles to disseminate information.
		Form connections based on shared intellectual interests.
		Pursue high ideals and abstract goals, sometimes at the expense of practical considerations.

SX5
	Core traits
		Experience emotions deeply, leading to passionate interests and relationships.
		Hold idealized views of love and connection.
		More openly display emotions compared to other Type 5 subtypes.
	Emotional landscape
		Anxiety about being misunderstood or emotionally exposed.
		Seek deep, meaningful relationships while fearing vulnerability.
		Struggle between the need for intimacy and the desire for independence.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Channel intense emotions into artistic or creative pursuits.
		Open up emotionally to a chosen few, seeking profound connections.
		Place partners or close connections on a pedestal, leading to potential disappointment.

SP6
	Core traits
		Exhibit a friendly and affable demeanor to foster alliances and ensure personal safety.
		Highly aware of fear, often seeking protection through relationships.
		Rely on trusted individuals for guidance and reassurance.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience persistent worry about potential threats and uncertainties.
		Frequently question their decisions and seek external validation.
		Suppress anger to maintain harmony and avoid confrontation.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Invest in forming close bonds to create a sense of security.
		Display indecisiveness due to fear of making wrong choices.
		Look to others for protection and affirmation.

SO6
	Core traits
		Adhere strictly to rules and authority to find security within structures.
		Demonstrate unwavering commitment to groups or ideologies they trust.
		Take on roles that emphasize accountability and service.
	Emotional landscape
		Anxious about being judged or rejected by their community or authority figures.
		Struggle with ambiguity, preferring clear guidelines and beliefs.
		Experience tension between personal doubts and the need to appear certain.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Strictly observe societal norms and expectations.
		Align with organizations or movements that provide a clear sense of purpose.
		Question new ideas until they align with established beliefs.

SX6
	Core traits
		Confront fears head-on, often displaying assertiveness or aggression.
		Engage deeply in relationships, seeking strong emotional connections.
		Challenge authority and norms as a means of asserting independence.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience a mix of fear and defiance, leading to emotional volatility.
		Struggle with trusting others, leading to testing behaviors in relationships.
		Mask insecurities with boldness, fearing exposure of true feelings.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Engage in daring activities to prove courage and suppress fear.
		Defend loved ones fiercely, sometimes to the point of overbearing behavior.
		Assess the loyalty and reliability of close connections through challenges.

SP7
	Core traits
		Pursue pleasure through tangible, material means, focusing on comfort and security.
		Identify and exploit opportunities to fulfill desires, often with a persuasive charm.
		Extend warmth and kindness to chosen individuals who align with their interests.
	Emotional landscape
		Distract from discomfort through indulgence in pleasurable activities.
		Anxiety about lacking resources or experiences drives their pursuit of abundance.
		Rationalize actions to maintain a positive self-image despite possible manipulative behaviors.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Build and maintain 'families' that provide mutual access to desired experiences or resources.
		Utilize charm and wit to influence others for personal gain.
		Prone to overindulgence in substances or activities that offer immediate gratification.

SO7
	Core traits
		Strive to be seen as selfless and morally upright, often suppressing personal desires to serve others.
		Engage in acts of service not solely out of genuine concern, but to reinforce a self-image of goodness and gain recognition.
		Adopt a persona of moral purity and self-sacrifice, sometimes masking underlying self-interest.
	Emotional landscape
		Desire to be perceived as altruistic and virtuous, leading to internal conflict when personal needs arise.
		Suppress personal desires, leading to hidden cravings that may manifest in indirect ways.
		Convince themselves of their selflessness, while unconsciously seeking validation and admiration.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Take on responsibilities and help others, sometimes beyond their capacity, to maintain their altruistic image.
		Deny personal needs and desires to avoid appearing self-centered.
		Look for acknowledgment and appreciation for their sacrifices, reinforcing their self-image.

SX7
	Core traits
		View the world through an idealized lens, often envisioning perfect scenarios.
		Experience emotions deeply, leading to passionate pursuits and relationships.
		Easily influenced by captivating ideas or individuals, sometimes leading to naivety.
	Emotional landscape
		Use imagination and fantasy to avoid confronting unpleasant realities.
		Anxiety about monotony drives the constant search for new and exciting experiences.
		Rapid shifts in mood based on the intensity of experiences or relationships.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Pursue new adventures, ideas, or relationships to maintain a sense of excitement.
		Place people or experiences on a pedestal, potentially leading to disappointment.
		Test limits in pursuit of deeper connections or heightened experiences.

SP8
	Core traits
		Prioritize autonomy and independence, focusing on securing personal resources and survival.
		Approach situations with a practical mindset, emphasizing tangible results over emotional considerations.
		Guard personal boundaries fiercely, often acting as a protector for themselves and close ones.
	Emotional landscape
		Tend to suppress emotions to maintain control and avoid vulnerability.
		Avoid relying on others, viewing dependence as a potential weakness.
		May struggle to empathize with perceived weakness in themselves or others.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Take decisive actions to secure needs and protect interests.
		Focus on gathering resources to ensure stability and self-sufficiency.
		Set and enforce strict personal boundaries to maintain autonomy.

SO8
	Core traits
		Demonstrate strong allegiance to groups or communities they identify with.
		Assume leadership roles to safeguard and advocate for their group.
		Challenge societal norms and injustices, especially those affecting their community.
	Emotional landscape
		Feel a deep responsibility to defend and uplift marginalized individuals.
		Remain vigilant against potential disloyalty within their social circles.
		Manage emotional displays to maintain authority and group cohesion.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Actively work to improve conditions for their group or community.
		Cultivate connections to strengthen their group's influence and security.
		Question and confront societal structures that they perceive as unjust.

SX8
	Core traits
		Engage with the world in a passionate and forceful manner, seeking deep connections.
		Desire to assert control in relationships, often testing boundaries.
		Exude a charismatic presence that draws others in.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience strong emotions that can lead to impulsive actions.
		Guard against emotional exposure, fearing loss of control.
		Develop intense attachments, sometimes leading to controlling behaviors.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Challenge partners to prove loyalty and strength.
		Address conflicts directly, often with intensity.
		Seek out experiences and relationships that offer emotional and physical intensity.

SP9
	Core traits
		Prioritize routines and physical comforts, often engaging in activities like eating, reading, or watching television to maintain a sense of peace.
		Grounded and concrete in their approach, focusing on tangible experiences over abstract ideas.
		Exhibit resistance to change, especially when their comfort is threatened.
	Emotional landscape
		Suppress anger to avoid conflict, which may manifest as passive-aggressive behavior or irritability.
		Tend to 'go to sleep' to their own emotions, avoiding awareness of internal discomfort.
		Overlook personal needs and desires in favor of maintaining external peace.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Stick to familiar habits and routines to create a sense of stability.
		Enjoy spending time alone, finding it more relaxing and grounding.
		React to external demands with passive resistance, often digging in their heels when feeling pressured.

SO9
	Core traits
		Focus on being part of a group, often working hard to meet the group's needs.
		Display sociable and congenial behavior, appearing fun-loving and friendly.
		Neglect personal needs by prioritizing the needs of the group.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience anxiety about not belonging, leading to overcompensation through group participation.
		Maintain a cheerful exterior while feeling a persistent sense of not belonging.
		Question their own existence and sense of self due to constant focus on others.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Engage in excessive work to support the group, often at the expense of personal well-being.
		Strive to maintain harmony within the group, avoiding personal conflicts.
		Take on numerous responsibilities to ensure inclusion and acceptance.

SX9
	Core traits
		Blend with significant others, adopting their attitudes, opinions, and feelings.
		Exhibit kind, gentle, and shy characteristics, often avoiding assertiveness.
		Seek a sense of self by connecting deeply with another person.
	Emotional landscape
		Experience a lack of personal boundaries, leading to difficulty distinguishing their own feelings from others'.
		Avoid expressing true thoughts and feelings to maintain harmony in relationships.
		Rely on close relationships to provide a sense of purpose and identity.
	Behavioral tendencies
		Tend to agree with others to avoid conflict, often suppressing personal opinions.
		Become deeply involved in relationships, sometimes losing their own identity.
		Display a lack of initiative, waiting for others to take the lead.

SOCIONICS: THE 16 SOCIOTYPES AND THEIR SEMANTIC ARCHETYPES

DICHOTOMIES AND ORGANIZING PRINCIPLES:

1. INTUITION vs SENSING  
	Intuitive types: Focus on patterns, possibilities, meanings, time flow, connections
	Sensing Types: Focus on present moment, concrete objects, physical sensations, material reality

2. LOGIC vs ETHICS (Thinking vs Feeling)
	Logical types: Base function on impersonal analysis, systems, facts and logical hierarchies
	Ethical types: Base function on personal or communal values, relationships, feelings and connections

3. EXTROVERTED vs INTROVERTED PRIMARY FUNCTION
	Extroverted primary function: Driven to external action and expression
	Introverted primary function: Driven to internal reflection and understanding

DUALS AND QUADRAS:

Socionics groups types into "Quadras" based on psychological compatibility:
- Alpha: SEI, ESE, LII, ILE
- Beta: IEI, EIE, LSI, SLE
- Gamma: ESI, SEE, ILI, LIE
- Delta: EII, IEE, SLI, LSE

Each type has a "dual" - the type with inverse function order within quadra, who serves as a positive counterpart and appreciated companion:
- EIE ↔ LSI
- ESI ↔ LIE
- ESE ↔ LII
- EII ↔ LSE
- IEE ↔ SLI
- IEI ↔ SLE
- ILE ↔ SEI
- ILI ↔ SEE

FUNCTIONS:

1. Lead Function: Explains the person's most comfortable thinking patterns, perspective on life, state of mind, and behavioral style
2. Creative Function: Explains the primary vector of application of the lead function, serving as main strategy to connect in society
3. Role Function: Explains the substitute and opposite to lead function approach to life and information metabolism
4. PoLR (Point of Least Resistance): Explains a mental domain, primarily causing feelings of frustration and inadequacy due to lack of personal understanding
5. Suggestive Function: Explains a mental domain, complimentary to lead function, serving as a to-go source of day-to-day soothing/entertainment
6. Activating Function: Explains the 'hidden agenda' will, lacking balance and temperance, but necessary in daily life
7. Ignoring Function: Explains a mental domain of neglected 'rival image' to lead function, serving as a insignificant or inefficient compared to lead one
8. Demonstrative Function: Explains the unconventional use of said function almost in a 'ridiculing' way to support more integrious creative function
